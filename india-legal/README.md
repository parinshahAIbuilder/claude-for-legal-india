# India Legal

Indian-law edition of Claude for Legal, built for in-house legal, company secretaries, CFOs, founders and their advisers.

| Skill | Use it for | Command |
|---|---|---|
| india-contract-review | MSAs, vendor and services agreements, NDAs under the Indian Contract Act, with stamp duty, arbitration, GST / TDS, DPDP and FEMA checks | `/india-legal:india-contract-review` |
| leave-licence-review | Office, retail, warehouse and co-working leases and leave-and-licence agreements: registration, stamp duty, lock-in, escalation, deposit, Ind AS 116 flag | `/india-legal:leave-licence-review` |
| sha-investment-review | Term sheets, SHA, SSA and articles for funding rounds: CCPS / CCDs, FEMA pricing, sections 42 / 62 process, investor rights, closing checklist | `/india-legal:sha-investment-review` |
| esop-scheme-india | ESOP scheme, resolutions, grant letters and employee FAQ under the Companies Act, SEBI SBEB, income-tax and Ind AS 102 | `/india-legal:esop-scheme-india` |
| board-resolutions-india | Board, circular and shareholder resolutions, explanatory statements, certified true copies and the MCA filing map | `/india-legal:board-resolutions-india` |
| fema-cross-border | FDI, downstream investment, ODI, ECB and GIFT City IFSC: eligibility, pricing, FC-GPR / FC-TRS / ODI / APR / FLA timelines and late-fee exposure | `/india-legal:fema-cross-border` |

Every output is a draft for review by qualified Indian counsel, a company secretary or a chartered accountant. It is not legal advice. Rules on stamp duty, FEMA, SEBI, MCA and tax change often; the skills flag timelines and rates that must be confirmed before anything is filed.

For the finance side (SEBI LODR disclosures, Ind AS 116, GST, TDS, CMA, NCDs), see [claude-for-indian-finance](https://github.com/parinshahAIbuilder/claude-for-indian-finance).
